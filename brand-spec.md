# Legatech local editorial design

This document describes the already-approved redesign being migrated from `../legatech redizajn2`. It is not a new brand proposal.

- Identity: the supplied lowercase Legatech SVG wordmark in `public/brand/legatech-black.svg`, used in the shared header and inverted to white in the footer. The supplied `legatech-t-small.svg` is the favicon source; `legatech-t-black.svg` is the full-size monogram. The older neon SVG in the exploration assets belongs to a different visual direction and is not used in these approved layouts.
- Colors: paper `#f5f1e8`, stone `#e9e3d8`, ink `#22241f`, muted `#626459`, signal `#b74426`, rule `#d6d0c5`.
- Type: Manrope Variable for display and body; IBM Plex Mono for navigation details, indices, and metadata.
- Layout: up to 1440px container; large split hero layouts; section rhythm based on whitespace and hairline rules; 4px radii for small controls and media surfaces.
- Motion: short navigation and hover feedback only; reduced-motion preference respected.
- Real imagery: project images in `public/projects/` and client logos in `public/clients/`. Do not substitute fabricated results or generic decorative imagery.
- Content language: Croatian. Keep routes, pricing details, form fields, legal copy, and project facts intact while changing presentation.
